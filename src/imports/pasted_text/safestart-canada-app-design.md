Design a modern, professional, and highly useful interactive web app called “SafeStart Canada”.

SafeStart Canada is a support platform for newcomers, immigrants, international students, refugees, and temporary workers in Canada. The goal is to help users quickly find trusted information and real human support for common problems they face after arriving in Canada.

Create a clean, friendly, trustworthy, and accessible web app UI. The design should feel safe, welcoming, multilingual, and easy to use for people who may be stressed, confused, or new to Canadian systems.

Main features of the app:

1. Homepage / Dashboard
- Hero section with the title: “Start safer. Settle faster.”
- Subtitle: “Trusted support for newcomers in Canada — healthcare, housing, legal rights, culture, safety, and community.”
- Search bar: “What do you need help with today?”
- Emergency quick buttons:
  - Medical emergency: Call 911
  - Housing crisis
  - Police or legal issue
  - Mental health support
  - Find a settlement worker
- Personalized cards based on user needs and location.

2. Category Navigation
Create clear cards for:
- Medical & Healthcare
  - How to find a doctor
  - Walk-in clinics
  - Health card information
  - Mental health resources
  - When to call 911
- Housing & Renting
  - Tenant rights
  - Rent deposits
  - Finding safe housing
  - Avoiding rental scams
  - What to ask before renting
- Police, Law & Authorities
  - What to do if stopped by police
  - Your rights in Canada
  - Reporting abuse or threats
  - Legal aid resources
  - Immigration-related support
- Culture & Daily Life
  - Canadian social norms
  - Workplace etiquette
  - School and university norms
  - Public transit behavior
  - Weather and winter preparation
- Jobs, Banking & Documents
  - SIN number
  - Opening a bank account
  - Resume basics
  - Work permits
  - Taxes
- Community Support
  - Local newcomer centers
  - Food banks
  - Language classes
  - Religious/community groups
  - Free local services

3. Smart Help Assistant
Design an AI-powered help box that asks:
“What situation are you dealing with?”
The assistant should guide users step by step and recommend trusted resources, documents, phone numbers, and nearby services.
Add safety note: “This app provides guidance, not legal or medical diagnosis. For emergencies call 911.”

4. Live Chat & Community Groups
Create a live chat section where users can talk to:
- Other newcomers
- Verified volunteers
- Settlement workers
- Healthcare professionals
- Housing advisors
- Legal aid professionals

Include group chat rooms:
- “New in Toronto”
- “Housing Help”
- “Healthcare Questions”
- “International Students”
- “Work & Jobs”
- “Ask a Professional”

Show verified badges for professionals and moderators.

5. Multilingual Support
Add a visible language selector in the top navigation.
Supported languages:
English, French, Persian/Farsi, Arabic, Spanish, Mandarin, Hindi, Urdu, Ukrainian, and more.
Include automatic translation for chat messages and articles.
Use an icon or label that says: “Translate this page”.

6. Resource Finder
Design a map/list page where users can find nearby:
- Clinics
- Hospitals
- Walk-in clinics
- Legal aid offices
- Settlement agencies
- Food banks
- Shelters
- Community centers
- Police stations
- Public libraries

Include filters:
Location, language support, open now, free services, online support.

7. Article / Guide Pages
Create readable step-by-step guides with:
- Simple language
- Icons
- “What to do now” checklist
- Required documents
- Common mistakes to avoid
- Trusted links
- Save/share button

Example article titles:
- “What should I do if my landlord asks for an illegal deposit?”
- “How do I get a health card?”
- “What are my rights as a tenant?”
- “What should I do if I need urgent medical help?”
- “How do I talk to police in Canada?”

8. User Profile
Create a simple profile where users can save:
- Preferred language
- City/province
- Immigration status type, optional
- Saved guides
- Saved resources
- Chat history
- Emergency contacts
- Checklist progress

9. Visual Style
Use a calm, trustworthy color palette:
- Deep blue or navy for trust
- Soft green for safety/help
- White/light gray background
- Warm accent color for important buttons

Use rounded cards, clean icons, friendly illustrations, soft shadows, and accessible contrast.
Make the UI feel like a mix of government reliability, healthcare clarity, and friendly community support.

10. UX Requirements
- Fully responsive desktop and mobile design
- Simple navigation
- Large readable text
- Accessibility-friendly
- Clear emergency warnings
- No overwhelming layouts
- Every page should help the user take action quickly
- Include micro-interactions such as hover states, active filters, chat message states, and saved resource feedback

Create the following screens:
- Landing page
- Main dashboard
- Category page
- Smart assistant page
- Live chat page
- Community groups page
- Resource finder map page
- Article/guide page
- User profile page
- Mobile version of the dashboard and live chat

Make the final design look polished, realistic, startup-quality, and ready for a portfolio presentation.
